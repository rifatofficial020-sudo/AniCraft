# Authors

People who have contributed to AniCraft. The repository starts from one initial commit
(`docs/release.md`), so the work before it is credited here.

* Tanish Manku: project lead, product direction and design.
* Koolskull: fill and stroke alpha in the colour picker (#354).
* lienatanael-dev: the live control port and its MCP bridge (#216).
* mypenc: skew in Free Transform (#176).
* Davit Matchakhelidze (devius): Georgian in the interface, with Noto Sans Georgian (#160).
* Mex (MMexicann): SVG path data, `Math.round` and `Math.min`/`max` signed zeros, affine inverses, documentation.
* NetterSilver (Silvertree2010): shape tweens that morph a raw shape's line with its fill, dash patterns, colour, easing and script-string parsing; an XFL nested-group regression test.
* jayzed80: flipped scales in Properties, CLI subcommand help and frame numbering; copy, cut and paste keys on the stage.
* Efe Şahinbaş (MacallanTheRoot): the MCP render caption.
* nocturney: localisation tests (Hebrew plurals, the product name).
* Vincent Agerbech (va-moviestarplanet): XFL group placement.
* scks1: raw drawings that align, distribute, turn and take strokes as one shape.
* Untoldent: the selection stipple, the scene breadcrumb, names selected in dialogs.
* gmrmad1: the zoom field, the Scale lock, playhead and scene-end fixes.
* Koolskull: group registration at the centre of the artwork.
* Kushagra Kumar (kk376): drawing with the camera layer hidden.
* Oskar Raszkiewicz (oskarraszkiewicz): the macOS menu row.
